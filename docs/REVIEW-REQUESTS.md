# Review requests after a won deal

When an opportunity is marked **Won** (by changing its stage, or when its quotation is converted to a job), SWK OS queues a message asking the client for a review. Nothing is sent by itself.

## Turn it on

1. Open Settings, Company, and enter your **Google review link** (it must start with `https://`). Leave it empty to keep the feature off.
2. The WhatsApp or email connection must be set up, because an approved draft is delivered through it.

## What happens

- A draft appears in CRM, **Message Drafts** with status *Pending approval*. WhatsApp is used when the contact or customer has a phone number, otherwise email.
- Someone other than the person who created it (the system creates it) approves the draft and presses Send. The CRM team gets a notification when a draft is waiting.
- One request per opportunity. Editing a won deal does not create another. A deal with no phone number or email gets no request.

## Not included

Monitoring Google reviews, reminders to clients who do not answer, and requesting reviews after lease signing (only opportunities are covered).
